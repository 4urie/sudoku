THE RULES

How to play Sudoku

01

Look for what is missing

Scan a row, column, or 3 × 3 box. Each must contain 1 through 9 with no repeats.

02

Choose a square

Click or tap an empty square and select a number. On a keyboard, use 1–9 and arrow keys.

03

Work it out

Toggle Notes for possible numbers. Erase an entry, request a hint, or check your work at any time.

The prefilled numbers cannot be changed. The puzzle is complete when every square is correctly filled.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Designed for a calmer puzzle break.

Sudoku Studio is a browser based Sudoku prototype with three difficulty levels, pencil notes, hints, a timer, and clear feedback. It works on desktop and mobile, with mouse, touch, and keyboard controls.
